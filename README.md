# 📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze mobile phone sales performance across different brands, cities, regions, and time periods.

The dashboard provides meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, helping users understand sales trends, customer preferences, and overall business performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall mobile sales performance.
- Identify top-performing mobile brands and models.
- Compare sales across different cities and regions.
- Track monthly and daily sales trends.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Monitor key business performance indicators.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset management and data preparation.

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Total Sales and Profit KPIs.
- Brand-wise sales performance.
- City-wise and regional sales analysis.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Top-performing mobile brands and models.
- Interactive filters and slicers.
- Dynamic charts for business analysis.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- Identify the brands contributing the most to total sales.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Analyze monthly sales fluctuations.
- Understand which mobile models perform best.
- Evaluate overall sales and profit performance.
